How many tacos per person?

Drop-off menus we list from DFW caterers on ezCater plan 2 tacos per person. That works when tacos are one part of a meal with rice, beans and other food. When tacos are the whole dinner, plan more.

Rule of thumb

  • Tacos are the main meal: 3–4 street tacos per adult.
  • Tacos plus sides (rice, beans, salad): 2–3 per adult.
  • Kids under 12: about 2.
  • Late-night snack at a wedding: 1–2 per guest who stays.

Plan about 410 tacos.

How many tacos for 100 guests?

About 300–350 if tacos are the main meal, or 200–250 with rice and beans.
