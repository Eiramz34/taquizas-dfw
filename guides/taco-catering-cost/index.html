How much does taco catering cost in DFW?

Of the 50 caterers we list, 22 publish some kind of price. Where a per-person price is published, it runs from $10 to $23 per person, with a middle of about $15.

What moves the price

  • Drop-off vs. on site. Delivered trays are the cheapest. A taquero cooking at your party, or a staffed buffet, costs more because you're paying for labor and equipment.
  • Minimums. Many caterers have a minimum order ($50–$300 for drop-off on ezCater, and up to $1,500 for full-service wedding catering).
  • Meats. Al pastor carved from a trompo, birria, shrimp and steak cost more than chicken or ground beef.
  • Extras. Rice, beans, aguas frescas, plates, servers and travel fees are often extra. One caterer charges $35/hour for an on-site attendant.

Questions

What is a taquiza?

A taquiza is a taco party: a taquero or caterer serves tacos, usually with several meats, tortillas, salsas, onion, cilantro and sides, for a set price per person.

Is a taco truck more expensive than drop-off?

Usually yes. A truck or taquero brings staff and equipment to cook on site. Drop-off trays are the lowest-cost option.
