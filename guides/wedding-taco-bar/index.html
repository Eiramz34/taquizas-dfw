Planning a wedding taco bar in DFW

Taco bars work two ways at weddings: as the main dinner, or as a late-night snack after the dancing starts.

As the dinner

Choose a staffed buffet or a taquero so guests aren't waiting at a drop-off tray. Add rice, beans and a salad or elote cups. Plan 3–4 tacos per adult.

As late-night tacos

A taco truck or cart for the last hour is a crowd favorite. Plan 1–2 tacos for each guest who's still there, and confirm the venue allows a truck and how long it can stay.
