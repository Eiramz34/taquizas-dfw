Ask before you book

  1. Is the price per person or per taco? How many tacos per guest does it include?
  2. Do you cook on site, or deliver trays? If on site, how much space and power do you need?
  3. Are servers, the taquero, plates, salsas and drinks included?
  4. Is there a minimum guest count, minimum order or travel fee for my city?
  5. Are you licensed and insured? (Many venues require proof.)
  6. How much is the deposit, and when is the final guest count due?
